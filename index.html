ABOUT AGRIPLAN

Making Farm Planning Easier

AgriPlan is an agricultural machinery selection and farm operation planning system designed to help farmers manage their farm information and plan their agricultural activities.

The system provides a simple way to organize farm operations, check machine availability, and identify suitable machinery based on predefined farm requirements.
